# MCS Grade 7 SHSAT Math Midterm: Annotated Answer Key

Companion to the attached Full-Length Math Module (Sessions 1–7 Cumulative Assessment): 50 core questions plus 4 very difficult bonus questions. Each entry identifies the answer, tested topic, and a brief solution. Questions **6, 10, 15, 20, 27, 33, 35, 37, 40, 46, and 48** are numeric-entry responses; question **26** is a fraction entry; questions **14, 31, 44, and 50** are select-all items, which earn credit only for the complete correct set with no extra choices; the others are select-one multiple choice. Bonus questions **51–54** are numeric entry and are scored separately from the core.

| 51 | **1494** | Ratios; remainders | With red \(7k\) and blue \(11k\), the total \(18k\) requires \(56\le k\le111\). The remainder conditions give \(k\) a remainder of 2 when divided by 9 and 3 when divided by 8, so \(k=83\): red 581, blue 913, total 1,494. |
| 52 | **12** | Mixture ratios | With original volume \(V\), removing 20% leaves \(0.24V\) concentrate and \(0.56V\) water; \(0.24V/(0.56V+18)=1/4\Rightarrow V=45\). The new mix is 10.8 to 43.2 liters; removing one third leaves 7.2 to 28.8. Then \((7.2+x)/28.8=2/3\Rightarrow x=12\). |
| 53 | **36** | Multi-stage rates; fractions of capacity | Stage 1 nets \(9-3=6\) L/min for 18 minutes, so 108 liters equal \(2/3-1/6=1/2\) of the capacity, which is 216 liters. The tank holds 144 liters at minute 18 and \(144-3(6)=126\) at minute 24. Stage 3 nets \(12-6=6\) L/min toward \((11/12)(216)=198\): \(72\div6=12\) more minutes, so \(24+12=36\). |

Enter **3000**, without the dollar sign. |
