Place-value control

Decimals: Move Digits, Not Dots

What multiplication by 10 really does
Every digit becomes worth ten times as much. The decimal point is the stationary boundary between ones and tenths.
Estimation guardrail
Before dividing, bracket the answer. If 2.4 times 10 is 24, then the number in 2.4n = 18 must be below 10.

Diagnostic repair · Q6

Clear a Decimal Without Changing the Equation

Q6 REPAIRLevel 3
2.4n = 18
Scale both sides
Multiply both sides by the same power of ten. The balance stays true.
Use whole-number division
Rewrite as 24n = 180, then divide.
Plug back
Check whether 2.4 × your value returns 18.
Decimal-placement trap
A value can look plausible while being ten times too small. Estimation and plug-back are two independent alarms.

Fraction · decimal · percent

One Amount, Three Languages

Fraction → decimal
Interpret the fraction bar as division: numerator ÷ denominator.
Decimal → fraction
Read the place value, write the matching power-of-ten denominator, then reduce.
Decimal ↔ percent
Percent means “per 100.” Scaling by 100 changes the name, not the amount.

Rectangle perimeter → area

G1: Boundary First, Inside Second

Perimeter finds the missing side
Write 2L + 2W = P. Isolate the pair of widths, then one width.
Area uses both dimensions
Only after both length and width are known should you use A = LW.
Unit check
Perimeter uses linear units. Area uses square units. The requested quantity decides the formula.
Diagnostic Q26 reframe
What tempting wrong answer would come from multiplying the perimeter by the length? How could units expose that mistake before calculation?

Missing-value statistics

S1: Mean Is a Total in Disguise

Unpack the average
Mean × number of values = total of all values.
Account for what is known
Add the visible values. The unknown is the difference between required total and known total.
Balance check
Insert the candidate value and recompute the mean. The equal-share picture must return.
Diagnostic Q36 reframe
Why is “add all known values and divide by five” incomplete when one value is missing?
