CAT Routine

Read the Relationship Before the Numbers

1 · Name
What two quantities are being compared? Write each label beside its number.
2 · Represent
Use a tape diagram, ratio table, or equation. Do not cross-multiply by reflex.
3 · Verify
Check that both quantities changed by the same factor and answer the requested quantity.
Before advancing
Say: “My denominator represents ___.” If you cannot finish that sentence, your ratio may be reversed or using the wrong total.

Ratio Language

Same Scene, Different Comparison

Part-to-part
blue : gray = 3 : 5
The two parts are compared directly. The total is not written.
Part-to-whole
blue : all = 3 : (3 + 5)
The second quantity includes every tile, including the blue tiles.
Common CAT trap
The numbers 3 and 5 are visible, but the requested denominator may be 8.

Equivalent Ratios

Scale Both Quantities by the Same Factor

Fast path · Scale factor
If one entry is a clean multiple, identify the factor and apply it to the paired quantity.
Flexible path · Unit ratio
Find the amount paired with 1, then multiply to the target. This is especially useful when the factor is not whole.
Structure check
Adding the same number to both entries usually changes the ratio. Multiplying both entries by the same nonzero factor preserves it.
Direct Variation

A Proportion Hides a Constant Multiplier

Constant of proportionality
k = y ÷ x
What stays constant?
The quotient y/x, not the difference y − x. Every point uses the same multiplier.
Graph test
Why must a direct-variation graph pass through the origin? What would a nonzero starting amount mean in the story?
Worked Solution · Geometry Link

Level 3: Ratio Parts Become Angle Measures

MODEL ALevel 3
The three angles of a triangle are in the ratio 2:3:4. What is the measure of the largest angle?
Step 1 · Count parts
2 + 3 + 4 = 9
The full 180° is divided into 9 equal shares.
Step 2 · Find one part
180° ÷ 9 = 20°
Each ratio unit represents 20°.
Step 3 · Rebuild target
4 × 20° = 80°
The largest angle corresponds to the largest ratio number.
Verification
The three reconstructed angles sum to 180°, so the parts and the triangle total agree.
Worked Solution · Direct Variation

Level 3: Find the Multiplier First

MODEL BLevel 3
The amount of paint, y liters, varies directly with wall area, x square meters. If 12 m² requires 18 L, how many liters are required for 28 m²?
Step 1 · Name the quotient
k = y ÷ x
Use liters per square meter because the question asks for liters.
Step 2 · Compute k
k = 18 ÷ 12 = 1.5
Every square meter requires 1.5 liters.
Step 3 · Apply to target
y = 1.5(28) = 42
The input changed; the multiplier stayed constant.
Reasonableness check
Since 28 is more than twice 12, should the output be more than twice 18? The computed value must respect that comparison.

Worked Solution · Reading Passage

Level 4: Track the Before-and-After States

Community Concert Wristbands. Before the gates open, the number of adult wristbands to youth wristbands is 5:3. During a recount, 24 damaged youth wristbands are removed. The remaining adult-to-youth ratio is 2:1. No adult wristbands are removed. How many adult wristbands were prepared originally?
Step 1 · Model original
Adults = 5n
Youth = 3n
One variable preserves the original ratio automatically.
Step 2 · Model new state
5n/(3n − 24) = 2/1
Only the youth count changes.
Step 3 · Solve & target
5n = 6n − 48
n = 48; 5n = 240
The question asks for adults, not for the scale factor.
State check
The original counts follow 5:3; subtracting 24 from youth makes the remaining counts follow 2:1.
Level 4 Challenge

Read for What Changes

Water-Testing Lab. A technician combines concentrate and distilled water in a ratio of 3:11. She prepares 84 milliliters of mixture. After testing, 12 milliliters of distilled water evaporate while the amount of concentrate remains unchanged.

What is the new ratio of concentrate to distilled water?
8Level 4
Enter the ratio in simplest form.
Pause points
How many equal parts are in the original mixture? Which part changes? Why would subtracting 12 from the total alone be insufficient?
Tempting shortcut
Do not subtract 12 from the ratio number 11. Ratio numbers are parts, not milliliters.

1:12–1:16 · Debrief

Diagnose the Decision Point

Meaning error
Part-to-part used when the question asked for part-to-whole; ratio order reversed.
Structure error
Only one quantity scaled; additive thinking replaced multiplicative thinking.
Target error
The work found one part or the scale factor, but the question asked for a group total.
Complete one sentence
“The earliest moment I could have caught my error was when I ___.” Name a specific check, not “be more careful.”
