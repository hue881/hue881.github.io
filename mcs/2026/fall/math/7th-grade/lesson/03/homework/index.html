SHSAT Transfer Set

8 questions · 20 min

Model the full situation before choosing an operation. The final line should contain only the requested quantity.

D1 · Part of a partLevel 3

A museum sold 680 tickets. Of the tickets, 35% were sold online. One fourth of the online tickets were student tickets. How many online student tickets were sold?

Tickets
D2 · Percent then fractionLevel 3

In a survey of 250 students, 72% chose an after-school activity. Of those students, 5/9 chose a sport. How many surveyed students chose a sport?

Percent number only
D4 · Ratio transferLevel 4

Percent number only

Percent number only
