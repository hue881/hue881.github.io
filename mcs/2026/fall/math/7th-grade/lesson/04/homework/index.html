D

SHSAT Transfer Set

8 questions · 20 min

Model the timeline before calculating. Separate percentage changes, fixed charges, and later credits.

D1 · Checkout timelineLevel 3

Appliance Pickup Notice. A $240 appliance is discounted 22%. Sales tax of 8.25% is applied to the discounted price. An $18 delivery fee is then added and is not taxed. A $12 reward for a future purchase does not reduce today’s charge. What is charged today?

Round to the nearest cent.

D2 · Compare orderLevel 3

A $160 item has two offers. Store A takes 25% off, then subtracts an $18 coupon. Store B subtracts $18, then takes 25% off. Which store is lower, and by how much?

D3 · Net effectLevel 3

A store raises a price 30% and later discounts the new price 20%. Relative to the original, what is the overall percent change?

D4 · Reverse balanceLevel 4

An account’s balance is $2,192 after earning 4.8% simple interest for 2 years. What was the principal?

The balance is principal plus interest.

D5 · Bonus ruleLevel 4

A $3,200 fund earns 3.75% simple interest for 28 months. A sponsor then adds a bonus equal to 15% of the interest earned. Find the combined interest and bonus.

D6 · Subscription noticeLevel 4

Renewal Order. An annual plan costs $480. At renewal, the price increases 12%. A $35 loyalty credit is then subtracted. Tax of 9% is applied to the amount after the credit. What is the final renewal charge?

Round to the nearest cent.

D7 · Multi-selectLevel 4

Select all expressions equivalent to decreasing x by 10% and then increasing the result by 25%.

D8 · Distractor analysisLevel 4

A student says, “A 20% discount followed by a 20% markup returns any price to its start.” Identify the exact misconception and use an original price of $150 to disprove the claim.

Correct final price
