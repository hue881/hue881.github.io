# Grade 7 SHSAT Math Program — Sequenced Syllabus
**Meeting time:** Saturdays, 80 minutes
**Fall term:** September 19, 2026 – December 19, 2026 (no class 11/28)
**Spring term:** January 2, 2027 – June 26, 2027 (no class 2/6, 2/13, 4/24, 5/1, 5/29)
**Total sessions:** 34 (13 fall + 21 spring) ≈ 45.3 instructional hours
**Target exam:** SHSAT, October 2027 (students test as Grade 8)

## 1. Course Description

This is a full-year foundations-through-application course in SHSAT mathematics for Grade 7 students. Because these students will not sit for the SHSAT until October 2027, the year prioritizes conceptual mastery and computational fluency over test-day cramming. Speed work is layered in weekly from Session 1 so that pacing becomes automatic rather than being taught as an emergency skill in the final weeks.

No calculators are used at any point in the course.


## 2. Learning Outcomes

By June 26, 2027, students will be able to:

1. Compute fluently with fractions, decimals, percents, integers, exponents, and roots without a calculator.
2. Translate multi-step word problems into algebraic equations, inequalities, and systems, and solve them.
3. Apply angle, triangle, circle, polygon, and solid-figure relationships to multi-step geometry problems.
4. Interpret tables, charts, and graphs, and compute mean, median, mode, range, weighted averages, and probabilities.
5. Produce exact numeric answers for grid-in (typed-entry) questions, where elimination and backsolving are unavailable.
6. Complete a full-length SHSAT math section within the official time limit at a sustained pace of roughly 85 seconds per question.
7. Maintain a personal error log and self-diagnose recurring error types (careless, conceptual, procedural, or misread).


## 3. Content Weighting

Session allocation mirrors the exam's own domain distribution rather than textbook chapter length.

| Domain | Approx. exam weight | Sessions allotted |
|---|---|---|
| Algebra & Functions | ~17 questions (highest priority) | 7 |
| Number Sense & Operations | ~14 questions (high priority) | 8 |
| Geometry & Measurement | ~11 questions (medium priority) | 6 |
| Statistics, Probability & Counting | ~8 questions (medium priority) | 4 |
| Assessment, review, strategy & pacing | — | 9 |

**Format note for instructors:** The digital SHSAT math section now runs approximately 50 questions with roughly 8 typed-entry items (about 5 numeric write-ins plus keypad entries). The legacy paper form was 57 questions with 5 grid-ins. Practice materials from before the digital transition still reflect the 57-question structure; adjust timing targets accordingly and tell students explicitly which form a practice set represents.


## 4. Standard 80-Minute Session Structure

| Minutes | Block | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 0–10 | Homework + error-log review; 3-question spiral warm-up | Retrieval practice on prior units |
| 10–35 | Direct instruction with worked exemplars | New concept, 2–3 exemplars minimum |
| 35–60 | Guided practice (mixed MC + grid-in), partner check | Application with immediate feedback |
| 60–72 | Timed drill: 10 questions / 10 minutes, no calculator | Pacing to ~85 sec/question |
| 72–80 | Drill debrief, homework assignment, exit ticket | Close the loop; identify next-week reteach |

Assessment days replace the instruction and guided-practice blocks with testing, then debrief.

**Weighting for progress reports:** diagnostic 0% (baseline only), quizzes 45% combined, fall benchmark 20%, full-length practice 25%, homework completion and error-log upkeep 10%.

**Growth targets:** +15 percentage points in raw accuracy from the 9/19 diagnostic to the 12/19 benchmark; +30 points from diagnostic to the 6/19 full-length section.


## 8. Homework and Error-Log Policy

- Weekly homework is 25 items (roughly 35–45 minutes), including 5 grid-in items on alternating weeks.
- Every missed question gets logged with: the question, the correct answer, the error type (careless / conceptual / procedural / misread), and one sentence on the fix.
- Error logs are reviewed in the first 10 minutes of each session and collected on quiz days.
- Optional packets are issued before each no-class weekend to prevent skill decay over breaks.


## 9. Materials

- Official NYC DOE SHSAT Specialized High Schools Student Handbook practice tests (Forms A, B, C)
- Digital-format practice sections for typed-entry familiarity
- Instructor-built topic drill sets, 25 items per unit lesson
- Student error-log notebook or shared spreadsheet
- Graph paper, ruler, and a timer; no calculators at any time


## 10. Summer Bridge and Year Two Preview

The summer 2027 bridge packet, issued 6/26/27, is built from each student's ranked weakness list from Session 34. Grade 8 instruction resuming in September 2027 should be pure full-length test simulation and pacing refinement — one section per week with item-level review — since the October 2027 administration leaves only about six weeks of runway. Any student finishing this course below 60% accuracy on the 6/19 full-length section should be flagged for additional summer intensive support rather than moving straight into simulation-only work.
