# Grade 7 SHSAT Math Program — Sequenced Syllabus 2026–27

**Format:** Saturdays, 80 minutes | **Sessions:** 34 (13 Fall + 21 Winter/Spring) | **Cohort:** 8 students (rising 8th graders)
**Target exam:** SHSAT, Fall 2027 (digital, computer-adaptive)
**Built on:** 9/19/26 Initial Diagnostic + *Enhanced Sequenced Study Plan: Grade 7 SHSAT Math (2026–27)*

---

## 1. Program Philosophy

1. **Diagnose → Target → Spiral → Verify.** Every session repairs specific 9/19 diagnostic items (Q-numbers listed per session).
2. **Front-load the weakest domains.** The diagnostic showed Geometry & Measurement at **30%** and Statistics & Probability at **39%**. Formal units stay in spring (domain-weighted order), but a **weekly Geometry/Statistics spiral strand (G1–G10, S1–S8)** starts 9/26 so these domains are never idle for six months. Spring units become a **second, deeper pass**.
3. **Train for the real test.** Starting fall 2026, the SHSAT is computer-adaptive: 50 math questions, you must answer each item to advance, and in Math **you cannot return** once you move on. Items include multiple choice, typed entry (fill-in), and technology-enhanced items (TEIs). Every drill in this program uses a **no-return, commit-and-advance** protocol — no "skip and come back," no end-of-test guessing sprees.
4. **Accuracy of format = points.** Typed-entry answers are one value, no % sign, no units, and answer the quantity asked.

## 3. The 80-Minute Session Template

| Minutes | Block | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0–10 | Warm-up + homework/error-log review | **Fixed 1-1-1 warm-up:** 1 prior-unit item + 1 Geometry strand item + 1 Stats strand item. Students **write the formula first**, then solve. |
| 10–35 | Direct instruction | 2–3 exemplars. Each exemplar names the diagnostic item it repairs. At least **one typed-entry exemplar** per session. |
| 35–60 | Guided practice (tiered) | Tier A / B / C sets. Partners must **state the answer format** ("one integer," "fraction in lowest terms") before checking. |
| 60–72 | Timed drill (10 items / 10 min) | **No-return protocol:** one item visible at a time; answer before advancing. Log elapsed time at item 5 and item 10. |
| 72–80 | Debrief, exit ticket, homework | Exit ticket includes one strand item. Errors logged by type (6 categories). |

**Weekly homework (same load all year):** 25 items = **20 unit items + 5 strand items (3 geometry, 2 stats)**. Tier C receives a worked example + 15 scaffolded items.

### Error-Log Categories (6)
1. Careless (arithmetic slip)
2. Conceptual (didn't know the idea)
3. Procedural (knew idea, wrong steps)
4. Misread (misread stem/figure)
5. **Format** (entered "70%", a list, units, or wrong form) — *new*
6. **Answered the wrong quantity** (found x when asked for 2x, all three integers when asked for one) — *new*

## 5. Test Mechanics & Pacing Protocol

**Pace:** about 85 seconds per question.

| Checkpoint | Target elapsed time (50-item section) |
|---|---|
| Finish Q13 | ~18 min |
| Finish Q25 | ~35 min |
| Finish Q38 | ~54 min |
| Finish Q50 | ~71 min |

**Commit-and-advance routine (replaces skip-and-return):**
1. Read the question and underline what is asked.
2. Around 2 minutes: eliminate impossible choices, pick the best remaining answer, and **move on** — you cannot come back.
3. Before typed entry: re-read the stem; enter one value, with no % sign or units, in the required form.
4. Never rapid-guess to "finish"; make a real attempt on every item.

**Stamina ladder:** 10-item drills (every session) → 25-item blocks by Session 10 → 50-item section at Session 33.

## 8. Required Formula & Fact Bank (memorize by 12/19)

- **Area:** rectangle lw; triangle ½bh; parallelogram bh; trapezoid ½(b₁ + b₂)h; circle πr²
- **Circle:** C = πd = 2πr
- **Volume:** prism V = Bh; cylinder V = πr²h; surface area via nets
- **Angles:** triangle 180°; polygon sum (n − 2)180°; regular polygon each = (n − 2)180/n; exterior = 360/n
- **Pythagorean:** a² + b² = c²; triples 3-4-5, 5-12-13, 7-24-25, 8-15-17 (and multiples)
- **Scale factor k:** length ×k, area ×k², volume ×k³
- **Percent change:** (new − old)/old × 100; multipliers (1 ± r)
- **Simple interest:** I = Prt
- **Slope:** (y₂ − y₁)/(x₂ − x₁)
- **Arithmetic sequence:** aₙ = a₁ + (n − 1)d
- **Stats:** sum = mean × count; shift +c → mean/median +c, range unchanged
- **Probability:** P(not A) = 1 − P(A); |A∪B| = |A| + |B| − |A∩B|
- **Facts:** perfect squares to 20²; cubes to 10³; primes to 50
