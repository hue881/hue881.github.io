## What We Cover This Year

The class mirrors the actual Digital SAT Math section: **44 questions in two 35-minute adaptive modules**, with the Desmos graphing calculator permitted throughout. We target the real domain weights — Algebra ~35%, Advanced Math ~35%, Problem-Solving & Data Analysis ~15%, Geometry & Trigonometry ~15% — and practice both multiple-choice and student-produced response (fill-in) formats.

### A Typical 80-Minute Session
1. **0–10 min** — Warm-up: four questions pulled from last week's error log.
2. **10–40 min** — Instruction on the day's skill cluster with Module-2-difficulty examples.
3. **40–65 min** — Timed drill: 10–12 questions at test pace (~1 minute 35 seconds each).
4. **65–75 min** — Debrief: answer-choice autopsy, Desmos alternate paths, fill-in entry rules.
5. **75–80 min** — Homework assignment and error-log entry.

## Expectations and Procedures

**Students will:**
- Arrive on time and ready to work; the warm-up begins at the start of the hour.
- Complete **30–45 homework questions per week** (one skill set plus one mixed set) and log them in the shared tracker before the next class.
- Maintain an **error log** for every missed question, classified as content gap, process slip, misread, or timing.
- Take all five full-length Math sections (9/19, 12/19, 4/10, 5/22, 6/19) under real timing.
- Show work on paper even when using Desmos, and ask questions during the debrief rather than waiting.

**Attendance and make-up work:** Email or text before class if your student will be absent. Missed materials are posted to Google Drive the same day; missed checkpoint modules are made up the following week. Attendance and homework completion are reported to parents at the end of each term (12/19 and 6/26).

**Behavior:** Respectful listening, no side conversations during timed work, and phones face down and silenced in bags unless a device activity is in progress. Repeated disruption results in a parent email, then a conference with the program coordinator.

**Advancing a domain:** ≥80% accuracy on the checkpoint module with no more than one timing-driven error. Students below the bar receive targeted remediation sets instead of moving on.


## Materials and Devices

**Bring every week:** a three-ring binder or folder for handouts, lined and graph paper, two pencils with erasers, and a printed copy of the SAT reference sheet (provided in week 1).

**Google Drive:** Every student receives access to their own folder where they can keep track of their homework assignments and tests. Please make sure your student can sign in to Google Drive before the first class — that folder is where all their work will live.

### Bring Your Own Device — Please Read

The SAT is now fully digital, so this class uses digital practice that mirrors the real exam whenever possible. **Students bring their own device. The school does not provide loaner laptops or tablets for this class** — there are none available to lend, so a device left at home cannot be replaced on the spot. School Wi-Fi is available and the bring-your-own-device policy is approved.

**Devices that run Bluebook**, College Board's official testing app: a Windows laptop or tablet, a Mac laptop, an iPad, or a **school-managed** Chromebook. A personal Chromebook or a phone will not work — Bluebook does not run on either. Screens must be at least 10 inches for laptops and 8 inches for tablets, and the battery should hold a 3-hour charge, since outlets in our room are limited.

**Before 9/19:** install Bluebook on the device your student will bring, sign in with a College Board account, and run the app's built-in device check. Practice results only save to the account when the student is signed in.

### If Your Student Has No Device

No device means no disadvantage in this class. Nothing in the curriculum is device-only:

- Every lesson, drill, and homework set is distributed on paper as well as in the Drive folder, and I grade fill-in responses by hand.
- On timed-drill days, students without a device pair with a classmate — one drives the screen, both solve and compare, then they switch roles the following week.
- For the five full-length sections, students without a device take College Board's official **paper (nonadaptive) practice test**, which comes with its own scoring guide and answer explanations. Scores are tracked on the same growth chart as Bluebook results.
- Desmos instruction happens on the projector, and I walk students through the same moves on a shared screen so nobody misses calculator strategy.
- Because the real SAT is taken on a device, students in the paper track should complete at least one Bluebook practice test at home, at a library, or during a school computer period before their first official test date. Email me and I will help arrange it.

Device use in class is **limited to the assigned activity** — no browsing, messaging, games, or off-task apps. Misuse ends device privileges for the rest of the term.
