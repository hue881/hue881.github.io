Guided path: the ratio changes

G2Level 4Guided solution path
A container initially holds red and blue beads in a ratio of 4 : 7. After 18 red beads and 6 blue beads are added, the ratio is 2 : 3. How many beads were initially in the container?
Choose or enter a response, then commit.
1 · Name one bundle
Initially: red = 4k; blue = 7k. The unknown initial total is (4 + 7)k.
2 · Update both quantities
Write 4k + 187k + 6 = 23. Thus 3(4k + 18) = 2(7k + 6).
3 · Solve, then return
12k + 54 = 14k + 12.
k = 54 − 1214 − 12. Find 11k, not just k.
Why the old total matters
Which numbers describe the initial mixture, and which describe the added beads? Where would you accidentally count the additions twice?

Guided path: average the journey

G3Level 4Guided solution path
A cyclist travels a route at 36 kilometers per hour and returns along the same route at 60 kilometers per hour. What is the average speed, in kilometers per hour, for the entire trip?
Choose or enter a response, then commit.
1 · Time is the hidden weight
Choose a one-way distance d. Travel times are d/36 and d/60 hours.
2 · Rebuild the definition
Average speed = total distance / total time:
2dd/36 + d/60.
3 · Cancel what you chose
Factor d out of the denominator:
21/36 + 1/60. Evaluate privately; retain km/h.
Why not the arithmetic mean?
On which leg does the cyclist spend more time? Why should the slower speed have more influence?
