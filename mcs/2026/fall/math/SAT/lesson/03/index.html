Distribution Is Area, Not Magic

The flashlight rule

A multiplier outside parentheses shines on every term inside. If one term stays dark, the expression changes value.

a(b + c) = ab + ac

Only matching units combine

Three meters plus two meters is five meters. Three meters plus two seconds is not five of anything. Variable parts are units.

Defend the boundary

Why can 6x combine with −3x, but not with −15?

SAT trap

Rational Equations

Cross-Multiply Only with Permission

Permission check

Cross-multiplication is shorthand for multiplying both sides by both denominators. It is designed for one fraction equal to one fraction.

Not a proportion
2/x + 3 = 7/5 has an extra term. Clear denominators deliberately; do not draw blind diagonals.
