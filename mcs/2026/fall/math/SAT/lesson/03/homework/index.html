Structure Before Symbols

4 prompts · 7 min

Do not solve unless asked. Name the legal move and explain what it preserves.

A1 · DistributionExplain

For −4(2x + 7), describe exactly how the factor −4 changes both terms. Why must the constant change sign?

A2 · Like TermsClassify

From 5x², −3x, 8, 11x², 2x, list the groups that may legally combine. Explain why x²-terms and x-terms are not alike.

A3 · EqualityAudit

A student changes 6x − 11 = 31 to 6x = 31. Identify the first invalid step and write the correct next line.

A4 · InvariantExplain

In one precise sentence, distinguish rewriting one side equivalently from performing an operation on both sides.

Translate and Defend

4 prompts · 9 min

Define the variable with units, write an equation, solve, and interpret the result.

B1 · Context ModelConstruct

A bicycle rental costs a one-time safety fee of $9 plus the same hourly rate. A 6-hour rental costs $57. Let r be the hourly rate in dollars per hour. Write and solve an equation for r.

B2 · MeaningExplain

In your equation from B1, explain which amount occurs once, which amount repeats, and why the coefficient is 6.

B3 · RepresentationChoose

Which equation represents: “Seven less than three times a number is 20”?

B4 · Distractor AutopsyAnalyze

For B3, choose one incorrect option and describe the exact language error that would produce it.
