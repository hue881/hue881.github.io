A

Read the System

6 prompts · 10 min

Do not solve unless asked. Read structure first: slope, intercept, coefficients, and equation relationships.

A1 · ClassificationReason

The equations 3x + 2y = 14 and 6x + 4y = 28 form what kind of system? State the number of solutions and the structural evidence.

A2 · ParameterAnalyze

For what value of k will kx + 6y = 15 and 4x + 8y = 20 represent the same line? Explain using a common scale factor.

A3 · Coordinate MeaningExplain

A system has solution (7, −2). Write the two substitution checks that must both be true for this ordered pair to solve the system.

A4 · Fast RecognitionAudit

Without graphing, explain why y = −3x + 5 and 6x + 2y = 4 cannot intersect.

A5 · Target QuantityTranslate

A question asks for twice the x-coordinate of the intersection. If the system solution is (a,b), what is the required response? Explain why b is irrelevant.

A6 · RepresentationConnect

Write a second equation that, with 2x − 5y = 9, creates a system with no solution. Your equation may not be identical to the first.

C

Build, Solve, Verify

6 problems · 16 min

For each problem, show the key algebraic step. Check the ordered pair in both original equations.

C1Level 1

What is the solution to the system x + y = 11 and x − y = 3?

C2Level 1

The system y = 2x + 1 and y = −x + 10 has solution (x,y). What is y?

C3Level 2

What is the x-coordinate of the solution to 4x + 3y = 1 and 2x − 3y = 17?

C4Level 2

For the system 5x + 2y = 16 and 3x − 2y = 8, what is the value of x + y?

C5Level 3

The graphs of 2x − y = 7 and ax + y = 5 intersect at (3,−1). What is the value of a?

C6Level 3

The solution to 3x + by = 17 and x − y = 1 is (5,4). What is b?

D

Translation Lab

4 models · 12 min

Define both variables, write the system, solve, and answer the exact question with units.

D1 · TicketsModel

Adult tickets cost $18 and student tickets cost $11. A group buys 26 tickets for $349. How many student tickets were purchased?

D2 · MixtureModel

A lab needs 30 liters of a 42% solution by mixing a 30% solution with a 50% solution. How many liters of the 50% solution are needed?

D3 · AccountsModel

Two savings accounts contain $8,400 total. One earns 4% annually and the other 6%. Together they earn $438 in one year. How much is in the 6% account?

D4 · Data PlanModel

Plan A costs $24 plus $8 per gigabyte. Plan B costs $54 plus $3 per gigabyte. At how many gigabytes do the plans cost the same, and what is that common cost?

Name the targetExploit structureUse Desmos deliberatelyVerify in context

G

Bonus Challenge

3 Level 4 + 1 Level 5 · untimed

These are extension problems. Show a defensible path; a correct answer without supporting work is incomplete.

G1Level 4

For real constants a and b, the system ax + 3y = 7 and 4x + by = 10 has solution (2,1). What is the value of a+b?

G2Level 4

A theater sold 240 tickets. Balcony tickets cost $18 and orchestra tickets cost $30. After 20 balcony-ticket purchases were upgraded to orchestra tickets for an additional $12 each, total ticket revenue was $5,520. How many tickets were originally orchestra tickets?

G3Level 4

The system (k−2)x + 3y = 9 and 6x + (k+1)y = 18 has infinitely many solutions. What is k?

G4Level 5

For constants p and q, every solution of 2x + py = 8 also satisfies qx + 6y = 24. The point (1,t) lies on both lines. What is the value of p+q+t?
