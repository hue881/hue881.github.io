Quadratic Structure

Q6: Roots Leave Fingerprints

Q6Level 3
The solutions to x² − 5x + 6 = 0 are r and s. What is rs?
A · −6
B · −5
C · 5
D · 6
Explain which coefficient controls the product.

Coefficient Fingerprints

For ax² + bx + c = 0:
r + s = −b/a    and    rs = c/a
The constant term stores the product because expanding a(x−r)(x−s) places a·rs in the constant position.
Do Not Mix the Fingerprints
How would the wording change if the test wanted −b/a instead? What single noun tells you which formula to retrieve?
