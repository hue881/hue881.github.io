Part 2 — Every Capstone Needs ONE AI Idea

That could be a trained classifier, a keyword chatbot, or an "AI character" whose replies change based on rules — as long as you can explain why it counts as AI.

🎮 Game example

An enemy that "decides" what to do based on your score — a simple decision tree.

🤖 AI app example

A model trained to tell two hand signs apart, or a chatbot that answers 5+ questions.

📖 Story example

A character that reacts differently depending on choices you type in — rules that "read" you.

Which of your ideas from Session 15–17 could become this AI piece?

🔨 Block 3
Guided Build — Your Project Plan · 20 min
Fill In as We Go — Follow the Teacher's Example

Everyone gets the same one-page project plan. We fill in Section 1 together, using the teacher's example project as the model — then you'll write your OWN.

✓ What does it do? One sentence, plain and simple.

✓ Who is it for? A sibling? A friend? Yourself?

✓ Which concepts will it use? Loops, conditionals, lists, functions, a trained model...

✓ What does "done" look like? Be specific — what will you show at the showcase?

Try it with me: the teacher models a Game example on the board, then you fill in your OWN sheet with your own track and idea.
