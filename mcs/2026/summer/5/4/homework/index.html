Part 2 · 15 min
Coding Challenge — List Your 3 Main Parts
Everyone — The Core Task

List the 3 main parts you'll build for your capstone. Think about the order that makes sense — what needs to exist first?

🌱 Tier A — Foundations

Draw each of your 3 parts as a small picture or icon, and label each with one word or short phrase. Ask a family member to guess what your project will do just from your pictures!

  • 3 parts drawn with labels
  • A family member guessed my project idea
⭐ Tier B — Core

Write each of your 3 parts as a full sentence describing what it does. Then write one sentence about which part you're most excited to build first.

  • 3 parts written in full sentences
  • Wrote which part excites me most, and why
🚀 Tier C — Accelerated

For each of your 3 parts, note which Python (or Scratch) concept it will use (loop, conditional, list, function, trained model...). Then flag which part is the riskiest and why.
