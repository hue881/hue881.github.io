📝 The Task
Design Your Own AI Model — On Paper
🎯 The Mission

Pick something you’d like to train an AI to recognize — two things that are different but maybe a little tricky to tell apart. You will name the classes, list examples for each, and think about what could go wrong.

Some ideas to get you started (or invent your own!):

  • Two dog breeds that look similar
  • Happy face vs. sad face
  • Recyclable vs. trash
  • Two kinds of fruit
  • Your own idea: something at home you could actually photograph

    • What will your AI recognize?

    Name your two classes and write 5 specific examples for each — be as precise as you can (not just “a dog” but “a golden retriever running in a park”).

    Tier C — Accelerated · Grades 6–9 · ~30 min
    🟣 Everything in Tier B, plus a tricky third class

    Complete the same 2-class, 5-examples-each worksheet as Tier B (use the Tier B boxes above), then add a third class that is intentionally hard to tell apart from one of your first two.

    Class 3 name (tricky — easily confused with Class 1 or 2):

    Explain exactly WHY Class 3 is tricky to tell apart from another class. What would the AI need to pay close attention to?

    Stretch: If you had to pick just ONE extra piece of information (besides a picture) to help the AI tell Class 3 apart, what would it be — smell, texture, weight, sound?
