📚 The 3 Scenarios
Read Each One, Then Decide

1The Homework Helper. A school uses an AI app to recommend which students need extra reading help. The app was trained mostly on data from students who had access to home internet and tutors. Some students without internet at home were never included in the training data, and the app now rarely flags them for help — even when they need it.

Was this AI fair or unfair?

One sentence why:

2The Photo Sorter. A photo app automatically sorts pictures into folders like "family," "food," and "pets." It was trained on millions of photos from all kinds of families, pets, and meals around the world, and it correctly sorts nearly everyone's photos, including yours.

Was this AI fair or unfair?

One sentence why:

3The Voice Assistant. A smart speaker was trained mostly on adult voices speaking clearly and slowly. It often fails to understand kids' voices, and it almost never understands anyone speaking with an accent different from the voices it was trained on.

Tier C — Accelerated · Grades 6–9
⭐ Stretch: Propose a Specific Fix

For ONE scenario above, propose a specific fix to the training data — not just "get more data," but exactly what kind, from whom.

Which scenario are you fixing?

Exactly what new data would you add, and from whom?

How would you know your fix worked? (What would you measure?)

No credit for "just add more data" alone — be specific about who, what kind, and how you'd test it.
