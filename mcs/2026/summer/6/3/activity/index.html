Today's Daily Rhythm
Your Mission Today

Today students build the smallest real version of their capstone, conference with the teacher, and check where the AI, data, or decision logic lives in the project.

Block 1 — Warm-Up (10 min, no screen)

Block 2 — Mini-Lesson: Tiny Working Slice (15–20 min)

Block 3 — Guided Build: Conference Prep (20 min)

Block 4 — Movement Break (5 min)

Block 5 — Open Build · Tiered (30–40 min)

Block 6 — Share-Out (10 min)

Key Ideas for Today
  • First working slice — the smallest real version of your project that actually runs
  • Teacher conference — a quick check-in on what works, what's stuck, and the next move
  • Mid-build AI check — pointing to exactly where the AI, data, or decision lives
  • Debugging — reading what actually happened, changing one thing, testing again
🤖 AI connection: AI/decision logic can be an if-then rule, a list of data (chatbot words, Teachable Machine examples), or a prediction/confidence score!

What to do

⭐ Tier B — Build the Tested Slice
What to do

Build your one testable piece from the mini-lesson (one game rule, one chatbot answer, or one story scene) until it actually runs start to finish.

  • One full testable piece runs without crashing
  • Tested it myself at least twice
  • Completed the conference prep questions
Describe your working slice
🚀 Tier C — Working Slice + Debug Log
What to do

Get your working slice running, then keep a debug log: every bug you hit, what you changed, and whether it worked.

  • Working slice runs reliably
  • At least 2 bugs logged with fixes tried
  • Can point to exactly where the AI/data/decision lives in the code
Debug log
🐛 Debug-day reminder: a bug is evidence that your project is becoming real. Read what actually happened, change one thing, test again.
