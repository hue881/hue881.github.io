Tier A — Foundations · Grades 1–3
🆎 Practice Saying Your Project Out Loud ⌛ ~30 min

Tonight, practice explaining your project to a family member, a stuffed animal, or a mirror — even a pet counts!

  • (10 min) Say out loud: what is my project called, and what does it do?
  • (10 min) Draw or write ONE thing you are proud of in your project.
  • (10 min) Say your whole intro 3 times until it feels smooth.

    • My project is called:

    One thing I'm proud of:

    ⭐ Challenge: ask your family member one question about your project and try to answer it!
    Tier B — Core · Grades 3–5
    ✅ Write & Rehearse Your 30-Second Intro ⌛ ~30 min

    Write out your full 30-second intro, then practice saying it out loud until it flows without reading.

  • (10 min) Write your 3-part intro below (what it is, the AI part, what you're proud of).
  • (10 min) Read it out loud 3 times, timing yourself — aim for about 30 seconds.
  • (10 min) Say it once WITHOUT looking at the paper. Note what you forgot.

    • What did you forget when you tried without the paper?

    ⭐ Challenge: time yourself with a clock or phone timer — were you close to 30 seconds?
    Tier C — Accelerated · Grades 6–9
    🟣 Bug Hunt Log + Polished Intro ⌛ ~30 min

    Do a deeper self-review: hunt for one more bug on your own, log it properly, then rehearse a tighter intro.

  • (12 min) Test your project 3 different unusual ways (weird input, fast clicking, etc.) and log any bug you find below.
  • (10 min) Write and rehearse your 30-second intro, including one sentence about the AI fairness check.
  • (8 min) Say the intro out loud twice, timing it to land close to 30 seconds.
    • ⭐ Challenge: ask a sibling or friend to be a “tough audience” and ask you one hard question about your project. Write their question and your answer.
